# Agent Fernandez brand guidelines

Structured like Anthropic's `brand-guidelines` skill, with Agent Fernandez's own identity in place of Anthropic's.

## Names

- **Public brand:** Agent Fernandez. The wordmark is set as AGENT FERNANDEZ.
- **Professional name:** Kevin Fernandez, REALTOR®. Use it in About, contact details, listing attribution, email sign-offs and legal lines.
- **Affiliation:** JRB Dream Team at Real Brokerage. It appears with Kevin's name in the footer, on listing pages, in the contact panel and in every email footer. The brokerage name is never smaller or less prominent than the team name.

## Wordmark

Text only, with no house icon or symbol: Albert Sans, weight 600, all capitals, letter-spacing 0.32em. It is charcoal on ivory, or ivory on charcoal. The favicon is an "AF" monogram in a charcoal square.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | `#F5F0E6` | Backgrounds |
| Linen | `#EAE2D4` | Bands, placeholders |
| Taupe | `#B3A592` | Hairlines and decoration only, never text |
| Taupe ink | `#6B5E50` | Secondary text |
| Charcoal | `#2F2D2A` | Text, buttons, footer |

There is no accent color: the photography supplies the color.

## Typography

- **Headings, addresses and prices:** Newsreader, weight 380–420, with optical sizing on.
- **Body and interface:** Albert Sans, 400 for text and 560 for labels and buttons.
- **Email (web-safe stand-ins):** Georgia for headings, Helvetica or Arial for body.

## Voice

Plain, warm and specific, in sentence case. Actions say what happens ("Request a valuation", "Ask about this home"). There are no superlatives, invented statistics or fabricated testimonials.
